A letter written with love

For Sheila

My mum. My memories. My forever love.

♥
↓   Read when you're ready   ↓

If I could have you back for just one day,

I wouldn't ask for anything extraordinary.

I wouldn't need a grand goodbye,

Or the perfect words to make sense of why.

I'd just want one more ordinary morning,

One more moment before the world kept moving.

One more conversation about absolutely nothing,

One more laugh that I didn't know I'd miss.

I'd tell you all the things I thought could wait,

All the little stories that time made too late.

I'd ask you questions I never thought to ask,

And wish I had known how quickly moments pass.

I wonder if you knew how much you mattered,

How your absence would leave everything scattered.

How the world could look exactly the same,

Yet nothing would ever feel quite the same.

Some days I still forget that you're gone,

And for a split second, life carries on.

Then reality finds me, as it always will,

And I learn all over again to live with the still.

I hope wherever you are, there is peace,

That whatever hurt you carried has finally ceased.

And though I'd give anything to have you here,

I hope you knew how deeply you were held dear.

"I am learning that grief is love with nowhere to go."

A thousand things I wish you could know,

So I'll carry your memory in all that I do,

And live a little more fully because I once knew you.

And if there is one thing your leaving has taught me,

It's never to take another heartbeat for granted.

To say I love you while there's still time to say it,

And never assume tomorrow is promised.

I miss you in ways that words cannot explain.

And if loving you means learning to live with this pain,

Then I'll carry it gently, for the rest of my days,

Because I'd rather have loved you than never known your name.

♥

Until we meet again, Mum.

You will always be a part of me.

Forever loved. Never forgotten.
